This is the first post. It doubles as a tour of what this blog can do: **MDX**, tags,
images, self-hosted media, and third-party embeds.

## Images

Images can live in `public/` and be referenced with plain Markdown:

![A self-hosted diagram from the public folder](/images/diagram.png)

Or they can be imported and optimized through Astro's asset pipeline. The hero image
above is a good example.

## Self-hosted media

Files you own go in `public/audio` and `public/video`. Both are wrapped in small
components so the markup stays tidy.

<AudioPlayer
  src="/audio/sample.mp3"
  title="Sample tone (440 Hz, 5s)"
  caption="Generated for testing, lives in public/audio/sample.mp3"
/>

<VideoPlayer

## Third-party embeds

Streaming services get a lazy iframe instead. Here is a YouTube video:

<MediaEmbed provider="youtube" id="dQw4w9WgXcQ" title="Example YouTube embed" />

Anything you do not want to host yourself can be embedded without adding weight to the
repo.

## Tags and sharing

Every post is tagged, and every tag gets its own page. Sharing links sit right below the
title, and the whole site publishes an RSS feed at `/rss.xml`.
